# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and this project uses
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [Unreleased]

## [0.3.0]

### Changed

- Rebuilt the interface in the Devigner style: cool near-black and paper
  themes, Geist and Geist Mono type, one blue accent, and a dot-grid stage with
  a breadcrumb pill.
- Every icon now comes from Devigner Icons, with filled (Bulk) drawings for the
  active page.
- New sidebar: settings search (press `/`), mono group labels with counts, and
  a highlight that glides between pages.
- Settings are grouped into panels of rows, each with its label, hint and
  control on one line.
- New controls from Devigner UI: liquid "goo" switches and sliding-pill tabs
  for the theme; pill buttons, hairline fields and mono keycaps.
- New Home hero with crosshair grid, terminal-style shortcut and a framed
  waveform; restyled history, model list, onboarding rail and recording dock.

## [0.2.0]

### Changed

- Complete interface redesign.
- New navigation: a grouped sidebar (Workspace, Preferences, App) with a new
  icon set, and the model, status and version moved into the sidebar.
- New pages: Home, Dictation, Audio, Languages, Output, Appearance and System.
  Settings from the old General and Advanced pages are spread across them.
- Settings are shown as a grid of tiles, with descriptions on each tile.
- New controls: check-mark switches, filled selectors and buttons.
- New Home page with your shortcut, current model and quick settings.
- New welcome flow with a dark side rail and vertical step tracker.
- Restyled model list and history timeline.
- New recording dock: a solid black island with a white waveform.

## [0.1.1]

### Changed

- New minimal interface: flat hairline cards, a flat sidebar, a bottom status
  bar, slimmer switches and rectangular buttons, in light and dark.
- Quieter onboarding with an animated waveform.

### Removed

- The donation row on the About page.

## [0.1.0]

### Added

- First release of Yap: a free, open source, offline speech-to-text app.
- New monochrome interface with light and dark themes and animated transitions.
- New logo, app icons, and tray icons.
